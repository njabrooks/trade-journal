# Stage 1: Signal Triage

## Selected Claim

### Claim 3: Physical World AI is the #1 Theme for 2026

**Level**: main
**Type**: thesis_candidate
**Category**: macro
**Tickers**: NVDA, QCOM, TSM
**Time Horizon**: medium_term
**Qualifier**: high
**Novelty Score**: ~0.75 (estimated, audit predates novelty enhancement)
**Consensus View**: Market is focused on cloud AI and hyperscaler capex; edge/physical AI is acknowledged but not yet the dominant narrative.

**Claim**:
The number one investment theme for 2026 is "connecting AI into the physical world" - encompassing edge devices (phones, cars, computers), NPUs for embodied AI, humanoids, data center buildout, and bring-your-own-generation (BYOG) power infrastructure.

**Evidence**:
- "Next year to me the number one theme is connecting AI into the physical world" (Transcript 09:36)
- Edge device upgrades needed for cars, phones, computers (Transcript 10:00)
- NPUs necessary for embodied AI, humanoids coming (Transcript 10:00)
- BYOG power trend in place solving power problems (Transcript 10:40)
- Colossus 2 building to million GPU cluster (Transcript 11:01)
- Tesla autonomous driving: no safety monitor in car, December 24th with Elon Musk, rolling out for public rides (Transcript 23:18)

**Reasoning**:
The shift from cloud-centric AI to edge/physical AI requires massive hardware upgrades across the economy. This creates PMI-positive demand for semiconductors, chemicals, power infrastructure, and manufacturing equipment - broadening the AI trade beyond hyperscaler capex.

**Backing**:
We are moving from the cloud to on-premise, on-site. "Bring your own generation. It is very physical. It is very hardware driven." (Transcript 37:23)

**Rebuttal**:
- Edge AI capabilities still nascent
- Consumer upgrade cycles may be slower than expected
- Humanoid timeline is highly uncertain
- Power constraints could limit deployment

**Supporting Evidence Claims**: claim-19, claim-20, claim-21
