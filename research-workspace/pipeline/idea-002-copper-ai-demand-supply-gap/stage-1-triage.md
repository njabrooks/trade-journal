# Stage 1: Signal Triage

## Selected Claim

### Claim 7: Copper Supply Is Unprepared for AI-Driven Demand Growth

**Level**: main
**Type**: view_candidate
**Category**: asset_specific
**Tickers**: COPX, FCX, SCCO
**Time Horizon**: long_term
**Qualifier**: high

**Claim**:
Copper supply is not prepared to meet the pace of AI-driven demand growth, making it a primary beneficiary of the data center buildout.

**Evidence**:
- "Copper is really the main metal there that benefits from this and the supply side of copper is not prepared to meet the pace of demand growth" (Transcript 17:50)
- Copper essential for data centers: grid buildout and grid reliability (Transcript 17:20)
- "One of the things that's most damaging probably to AI is not having great stability" - copper critical for grid reliability (Transcript 17:20)
- Energy demand from AI could grow at pace above GDP (Transcript 17:10)

**Reasoning**:
Data centers require massive copper for grid infrastructure and reliability. Mining supply is constrained by long development timelines (7-10 years from discovery to production). When demand grows faster than supply can respond, prices must rise to ration demand.

**Backing**:
Copper mine supply has chronically disappointed expectations due to grade declines, water scarcity, and permitting challenges. AI demand represents incremental demand on already-tight market.

**Rebuttal**:
- Copper substitution (aluminum in some applications) could ease shortage
- Recycling could increase supply response
- Chinese demand could soften, offsetting AI demand growth

**Supporting Evidence Claims**: claim-20, claim-22
**Rebutting Evidence Claims**: None identified
