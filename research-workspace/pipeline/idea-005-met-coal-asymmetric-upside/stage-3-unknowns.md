## Top 3 Unknowns (Detailed Analysis)

### Unknown 1: Current HCC/AMR Valuations — How Much Has Already Re-Rated?

**Decision Impact**: HIGH — This is the single most important unknown. The thesis claims 4x EV/EBITDA with 50%+ upside. If HCC and AMR have already re-rated to 5.5-6x following the 2025 steel rally (SLX +46%), the asymmetry is gone and the thesis fails on entry timing, not on fundamentals. This unknown can flip the entire trade from "go" to "too late."

**Resolution Type**: empirical

**Externally Resolvable**: yes — public market data, SEC filings, consensus estimates

**Kill Condition**:
- HCC or AMR currently trading above 5.5x forward EV/EBITDA on consensus 2026 estimates
- Both stocks up >40% from pre-2025 steel rally levels with sell-side upgrades already widespread
- Insider selling at either company in the last 90 days exceeding $5M aggregate

**Conviction Increase Condition**:
- HCC and AMR still trading below 4.5x forward EV/EBITDA despite 2025 steel rally
- Consensus estimates flat or still declining (market hasn't caught up to improved fundamentals)
- No or minimal sell-side upgrades in past 6 months (idea is still uncrowded)
- Insider buying or flat holdings (management confident in cash flow sustainability)

**Recommended Sources**:
- **Bloomberg/FactSet**: Current EV/EBITDA, forward estimates, price history since Jan 2025
- **HCC 10-K/10-Q (SEC EDGAR)**: Most recent quarterly production costs, realized pricing, buyback activity
- **AMR 10-K/10-Q (SEC EDGAR)**: Same + buyback remaining authorization and pace
- **Sell-side coverage**: Count of Buy/Hold/Sell ratings, date of most recent upgrades
- **SEC Form 4 filings**: Insider transaction history for HCC and AMR

**Estimated Effort**: 3-4 hours

**Research Queries**:
1. What are HCC and AMR's current forward EV/EBITDA multiples on consensus 2026E and 2027E?
2. How much have HCC and AMR stock prices moved since January 2025? How does this compare to SLX?
3. What is the current sell-side consensus (# Buy/Hold/Sell) and has it shifted in the past 6 months?
4. What is AMR's remaining buyback authorization and what was the repurchase pace in the last 2 quarters?
5. Have HCC or AMR insiders been net buyers or sellers in the past 90 days?

### Unknown 2: China Steel Production Trajectory in 2026

**Decision Impact**: HIGH — China is 55% of global steel production. The thesis depends on BF-BOF utilization staying above 75% globally. Even a 5% decline in Chinese crude steel output would overwhelm India's +9% growth in absolute volume terms (~50 Mt decline vs. ~14 Mt increase). This is the most important macro variable for seaborne met coal pricing.

**Resolution Type**: empirical

**Externally Resolvable**: partially — historical data is excellent (World Steel Association, NBS), but forward trajectory depends on Beijing stimulus decisions which are not fully predictable. However, recent monthly trends and announced policy direction provide strong signals.

**Kill Condition**:
- Chinese crude steel production declining >5% YoY for 3 consecutive months (NBS monthly data)
- China property starts declining >15% YoY with no offsetting infrastructure acceleration
- Australian HCC benchmark below $180/ton and trending lower
- China rebar futures in sustained contango (indicating oversupply expectations)

**Conviction Increase Condition**:
- Chinese crude steel production flat or growing in H1 2026 (infrastructure offsetting property weakness)
- Beijing announces major infrastructure stimulus package specifically targeting steel-intensive sectors
- Australian HCC benchmark above $220/ton and stable or rising
- Indian crude steel production growing >8% YoY (confirming demand diversification away from China dependency)

**Recommended Sources**:
- **World Steel Association**: Monthly crude steel production by country (released ~3 weeks after month-end)
- **China NBS**: Monthly industrial production, fixed asset investment, property starts
- **Bloomberg Commodities**: Australian HCC index, China rebar futures curve
- **IEA/EIA**: Global steel demand forecasts, semi-annual updates
- **S&P Global Commodity Insights**: Seaborne met coal market reports

**Estimated Effort**: 4-5 hours

**Research Queries**:
1. What was China's crude steel production in Q4 2025 and January 2026? YoY trend?
2. Are China property starts still declining, and is infrastructure/manufacturing offsetting?
3. What is the current Australian HCC spot price and the forward curve shape?
4. What are the latest WSA and IEA forecasts for 2026 global steel production?
5. Is India's steel production tracking the +9% forecast? What are the latest monthly figures?

### Unknown 3: HCC/AMR Cost Competitiveness vs. Australian Producers

**Decision Impact**: HIGH — Even if the met coal demand thesis is correct, the investment fails if U.S. producers lose share to lower-cost Australians. The thesis implicitly assumes HCC and AMR can participate in global demand growth, but if their FOB + freight cost to Asian buyers is $30-50/ton above Australian delivered cost, they are marginal suppliers who only benefit at the very top of the price cycle. This determines whether the right equity expression is U.S. pure-plays or Australian miners (BHP Met Coal, Stanmore, Whitehaven).

**Resolution Type**: empirical + industry

**Externally Resolvable**: yes — company filings disclose cash costs per ton, and freight rate data is available. The key comparison is all-in delivered cost to Asian ports.

**Kill Condition**:
- HCC/AMR cash cost per ton >$120 FOB mine (making them uncompetitive below $200/ton HCC benchmark)
- U.S. met coal export volumes declining QoQ despite rising global demand (share loss)
- Freight rates from Hampton Roads/Baltimore to Asia >$40/ton above Newcastle/Hay Point to Asia
- HCC or AMR reporting declining export volumes or shifting to lower-margin domestic sales

**Conviction Increase Condition**:
- HCC/AMR cash cost per ton <$100 FOB mine (competitive at $180+ benchmark)
- U.S. met coal exports growing in line with or above global demand growth
- Premium HCC grade from Alabama/Appalachia commanding $15-25/ton quality premium over standard Australian HCC
- HCC/AMR contract book showing increased Asian offtake commitments for 2026-2027

**Recommended Sources**:
- **HCC 10-K/10-Q**: Cash cost per ton, sales volumes by region (domestic vs. export), realized pricing
- **AMR 10-K/10-Q**: Same metrics, plus production guidance
- **EIA Coal Markets**: U.S. coal export data by destination
- **Argus/Platts**: Freight rate assessments for coal routes (Hampton Roads → Asia vs. Newcastle → Asia)
- **BHP annual report / Stanmore quarterly**: Comparison cost benchmarks for Australian met coal

**Estimated Effort**: 3-4 hours

**Research Queries**:
1. What are HCC and AMR's latest reported cash cost per ton (FOB mine and FOB port)?
2. What percentage of HCC/AMR sales are export vs. domestic? Is the export share growing?
3. What is the current freight rate differential between U.S. East Coast → Asia vs. Australia → Asia coal routes?
4. Do HCC/AMR premium Alabama HCC grades command a quality premium over standard Australian HCC? How much?
5. What are BHP Met Coal and Whitehaven's comparable cash costs per ton?

Kill conditions are clear and measurable for all three. The research is asymmetric: ~10-13 hours of total effort can either kill the idea early (saving capital misallocation) or raise conviction meaningfully (from 0.50 to 0.65+).

The critical sequencing is: **check Unknown 1 (valuation) first** — if HCC/AMR have already re-rated above 5.5x, the trade is dead regardless of how strong the fundamental thesis is. This is 3-4 hours of work that can save the remaining 7-9 hours.
