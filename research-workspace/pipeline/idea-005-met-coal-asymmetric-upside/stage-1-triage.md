# Stage 1: Signal Triage

## Selected Claim

### Claim 2: Met Coal Equities Offer Asymmetric Upside Due to Strong Global Steel Demand and No Viable Substitutes for Coking Coal in Blast Furnaces

**Level**: main
**Type**: asset_thesis_candidate
**Category**: asset_specific
**Tickers**: HCC, AMR, METC, SXC
**Time Horizon**: medium_term
**Qualifier**: high
**Novelty Score**: 0.50
**Consensus View**: Market recognizes met coal's essential role in steelmaking but prices in long-term structural decline from EAF substitution and eventual green steel (H2-DRI). Most investors underweight coal equities due to ESG mandates regardless of fundamental quality.

**Claim**:
Pure-play metallurgical coal producers (HCC, AMR) are the highest-conviction way to express a bullish steel demand view. Met coal has no commercially viable substitute in blast furnace steelmaking, 70% of global steel requires it, and India's +9% steel demand growth is a direct near-term catalyst. Depressed valuations (sector at 4.1x EV/EBITDA) amplify upside.

**Evidence**:
- 70% of global steel made via BF-BOF process requiring met coal; every tonne requires ~780 kg of coking coal (Section 9)
- No commercially viable substitute exists for coke in blast furnaces (Section 4, 9)
- SLX ETF +46% YTD 2025 signaling strong steel demand (Section 2)
- India forecast +9% steel demand 2025-2026, imports most met coal from Australia (Section 5, 9)
- HCC is 100% pure-play met coal, exports to ArcelorMittal/Nippon Steel (Section 9)
- AMR is 100% met coal after spinning thermal, largest U.S. met coal producer by volume (Section 9)
- Sector trades at 4.1x EV/EBITDA (Section 2)
- Steel industry consumes ~75% of global coking coal production (Section 9)

**Reasoning**:
Met coal's irreplaceability in BF-BOF steelmaking creates inelastic demand as long as blast furnaces operate. With 70% of global steel production dependent on this process and India driving incremental growth, volume demand is structurally supported. The ESG-driven capital exodus has compressed valuations to levels that don't reflect the cash flow generation at current commodity prices.

**Backing**:
Global BF-BOF production runs ~1.3B tonnes annually consuming ~755 Mt coking coal. Cleveland-Cliffs is expanding BF capacity (not converting to EAF), signaling that major steelmakers see continued BF economics. Historical precedent: met coal equities have outperformed in every steel upcycle due to operating leverage.

**Rebuttal**:
- EAF substitution is accelerating (currently 30% global, 70% U.S.) and chips away at met coal's addressable market
- China steel demand could weaken if property sector contraction deepens
- Green steel technology (H2-DRI) is a long-term structural threat, potentially viable by 2035+
- Global recession would tank steel demand and met coal prices simultaneously
- Australian met coal producers (BHP, Stanmore) have lower cost structures than U.S. producers

**Supporting Evidence Claims**: claim-14, claim-15, claim-16, claim-17, claim-18
**Rebutting Evidence Claims**: claim-19, claim-20
