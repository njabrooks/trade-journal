# Money, Civil & International War, Minneapolis, and Beyond—in Perspective

## Summary

Ray Dalio argues we are on the brink of moving from Stage 5 (pre-breakdown of existing orders) to Stage 6 (breakdown of orders) in his Big Cycle theory. The last time this happened was 1930-1945, leading to the post-war order we're now seeing break down.

## The Classic Toxic Mix

Three forces that bring big internal conflicts:
1. Country in bad financial shape (big debt/obligations)
2. Large income, wealth, and values gaps
3. Severe negative economic shock

## Key Indicators of Stage 5 → Stage 6 Transition

- Government has large deficits creating more debt than buyers want
- Governments either raise taxes/cut spending OR print money (devalues currency)
- Those with wealth flee high-tax jurisdictions
- Largest wealth gaps + worst income declines = greatest conflicts
- US states/cities with highest per capita income tend to be most indebted with largest wealth gaps (SF, Chicago, NYC, CT, IL, MA, NY, NJ)

## Populism and Extremism

- Populists emerge from disorder and discontent
- They fight for the common man against elites
- Can be left (communism) or right (fascism)
- Confrontational rather than collaborative
- 1930s: four democracies turned into autocracies

## Historical Pattern

Last monetary/political/geopolitical order breakdown: 1930-1945
Current situation mirrors that period
Without course correction, heading toward Stage 6 breakdown
