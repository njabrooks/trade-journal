## Claude Provider Adapter

1. Require one explicit Trade Journal research insight UUID. From the Trade Journal root, use Claude's repository command runner for `npx tsx scripts/research-pipeline.ts --describe --insight-id <uuid>` to obtain the complete expand-phase lifecycle, or `npx tsx scripts/research-pipeline.ts --evaluate <file|->` to evaluate supplied exact stage material. Validate any stored result with `npx tsx scripts/research-pipeline.ts --validate-result <file|->`.
2. Preserve authority exactly. Notes/Tana owns capture, source material, and Toulmin extraction. Trade Journal owns promoted claims, research publication state, governed relationships, theses, unresolved Decision Items, and journal audit. The aggregate coordinates stage outcomes; it does not acquire any stage's authority.
3. Resolve the Registry-locked `claims-synthesis`, `research-publication`, and `belief-research-relation` Capabilities and use their existing preparation and result validators. Preserve exact package, source, context, result, provenance, qualifier, rebuttal, ambiguity, authorization, audit, idempotency, and rollback bindings. Never reimplement or loosen a stage validator.
4. Exact `(sourceInsightId, sourceClaimId)` provenance is identity. Reuse provenance-bearing claims. Holdings are not an information gate. Tickers, keywords, and provider recommendations are never semantic proof of claim identity or thesis bearing.
5. Report every stage exactly as `incomplete`, `unavailable`, `stale`, `refused`, `failed`, `judgment_required`, or `ready`. Preserve an upstream non-ready result without inventing a retry or partial success. Retry only canonically identical input using the aggregate digest as the retry key.
6. Legacy stage entry points remain active: pipeline-status, stage-1-init-idea, stage-2-formalize-thesis, stage-3-map-unknowns, stage-4a-prep-desktop-research, stage-4a-research-unknown, stage-4b-synthesize-evidence, stage-5-express-thesis, advance-or-kill, and graduate-pipeline-idea. Report them as explicitly unmigrated; do not alter their files, mirrors, invocation, or active discovery in this expand phase.
7. The aggregate result must remain bounded and use `execution: { mode: "aggregate_coordination_only", writes: [] }`. This adapter must not use ad-hoc SQL, Supabase MCP writes, direct API mutation, or generic writes; must not create or publish claims or relationships; and must not change status, resolve a Decision Item, mutate a strategy or position, or place or stage a trade.
8. If the exact research-publication stage returns `judgment_required`, the only named persistence operation is `scripts/ops/publish-research.ts --stdin`. If the exact belief-research-relation stage returns its recording judgment boundary, the only named persistence operation is `scripts/ops/record-belief-research-relation.ts --stdin`. Never invoke either operation, mint authorization, infer acceptance, or describe readiness as persistence from aggregate authority.
9. The aggregate has no scheduler, credential, provider-discovery, production-data, status, decision-resolution, signal-configuration, strategy, position, order, or trade authority. Unavailable or sandbox-denied prerequisites remain explicit outcomes with no fallback write path.
10. Unattended execution is conditional only for the aggregate's read-only describe, evaluate, and validate modes. Genuine judgment, any accepted write, and all legacy stage eligibility remain governed by their exact stage boundary; the aggregate always returns `writes: []`.
