# AI RESEARCH SKILL ARCHITECTURE: FROM PODCAST SIGNAL TO EQUITY IDEAS

## 1. Signal Identification (Human-Led, AI-Assisted)

**Finding what matters**

This process starts with a human listening to a podcast, attending a conference, reading a paper, or speaking directly with someone. Humans are uniquely good at recognizing when a comment or idea connects to something larger, breaks consensus, or fits into a broader investment narrative. That judgment can't be automated.

AI supports this step by organizing notes, transcribing content, and making sure no important context is lost but the human identifies the investment dot. AI helps capture it, not discover it.

## 2. Thesis Structuring (AI-Led Execution)

**Turning intuition into an institutional framework**

Once the human has identified a promising idea, AI takes over the heavy lifting. The goal here is no longer nuance it's structure. AI expands the rough insight into a complete investment framework, mapping market size, supply chains, competitive dynamics, incentives, and failure modes.

This is disciplined, repeatable work where AI excels. The human steps back while AI turns intuition into a clear, analyzable thesis.

## 3. Deep Research (AI at Scale)

**Building depth and conviction**

With a structured thesis in place, AI is tasked with doing what humans struggle to do efficiently: deep, exhaustive research. Multiple long-form, fact-heavy reports are generated, each taking a different angle and reasoning approach.

AI reads broadly, connects domains, analyzes risks and second-order effects, and begins mapping themes to specific companies. The human's role is minimal here: oversight, not execution. This phase is about thoroughness, not creativity.

## 4. Research Consolidation (AI-Led Synthesis)

**Reducing bias and resolving contradictions**

Now multiple deep research reports are brought together. AI compares them, identifies overlap and disagreement, weighs evidence, and resolves contradictions. Instead of relying on one perspective, this step creates a probabilistic, evidence-weighted view.

AI is ideal here because it has no ego, no attachment to a single narrative, and can hold competing truths simultaneously. The output is a single, consolidated picture that is far more robust than any individual report.

## 5. Final Filtering & Portfolio Decision (Human-Led, AI-Supported)

**Increasing the probability of success**

The final step returns decision-making to the human. This is where judgment matters most. The human evaluates sentiment, crowding, factor exposure, macro conditions, timing, and portfolio fit all contextual elements that require experience and intuition.

AI continues to support by gathering and organizing data such as earnings revisions, sentiment indicators, earnings call summaries, and other inputs. But the final call is human. The goal is not prediction it is to increase the probability of success by integrating human judgment with AI-powered analysis.

## The Unifying Principle

**Humans decide what matters. AI does the work required to understand it. Humans decide how to act.**

This system is not about replacing analysts it's about pairing human insight with AI scale to make better, more disciplined investment decisions.
